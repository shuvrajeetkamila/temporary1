Choose your ride

Walk, drive, sail or fly — the same continuous world.

🚆 Local trains: stand on a platform when a train stops and press 🚪(E) to board; press again at the next station to alight.

How to play

Move: joystick (bottom-left) or W A S Darrows. Run: Shift.
Throttle: W/S or ▲▼ buttons. Heli/plane climb: R/F or hold ▲▼.
Enter/exit/board train: E. Brake: Space.
Map: M · Teleport: T · Vehicles: V · Zoom: wheel / pinch.

Tips

• Roads are fast, fields are slow, water stops cars. Boats need 0.5 m of draft.
• Planes: throttle up on a runway, pull back (R) past ~120 km/h.
• Monsoon (Jun–Sep) brings rain, lightning and flooded shoulders. Tides move every 6h.
• Discover all 780 locations from the prompt's dossiers — tap any dot on the map.

About this realm

An open-world recreation of the Mumbai–Navi Mumbai island region, built from the Mumbai_NaviMumbai_IslandGame_Prompt specification: 780 location dossiers, ten regions, tidal creeks, suburban railways, the JNPT port belt, monsoon weather and semi-diurnal tides.

Design honouring the spec: real coordinates per dossier · layered minimap & teleport system (Part A) · teleport fairness charges (Part B) · per-vehicle physics (Part C) · NPC archetypes & crowd rhythms (Part D) · seasons, monsoon, tides, wildlife (Part E) · open-data credit (Part F).
